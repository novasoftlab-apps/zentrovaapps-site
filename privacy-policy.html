1. Who we are and what this covers

This Privacy Policy is issued by Zentrovaapps (SMC-Private) Limited ("Zentrovaapps", "we", "us" or "our"), a Single Member Private Limited company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2017 of Pakistan and registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan under Incorporation Number 0344416.

This policy applies to every mobile application we publish on Google Play (each an "App", together the "Apps"), including our range of universal smart-TV and streaming-device remote control applications, and to this website at zentrovaapps.com. Where a particular App handles data differently from what is described here, that difference will be disclosed inside the App itself and in its Google Play Data safety section.

We are the data controller for the limited processing described in this policy. By downloading, installing or using an App, you confirm that you have read and understood this policy.

2. Privacy at a glance

The short version. Our Apps do not require an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or address. We do not operate servers that receive or store your personal information. The only data that leaves your device is the technical and advertising data collected by Google AdMob to serve ads, and the purchase data processed by Google Play Billing when you buy something. Everything else — your paired devices, your settings and your favourites — stays on your phone.

3. What we do not collect

To be explicit, our Apps do not collect, request, transmit or store:

  • Your name, email address, postal address, phone number or date of birth;
  • User accounts, passwords or authentication credentials — there is no sign-up or login;
  • Precise or approximate geographic location from GPS or location services;
  • Your contacts, calendar, call logs, SMS messages or photo and media library;
  • Audio or video recordings, except where an App expressly offers a voice-search feature that you actively trigger, as described in section 6;
  • Health, financial, biometric or other special-category personal data;
  • A record of what you watch, which channels you tune to, or the content displayed on your television.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with data brokers.

4. Information collected automatically

5. Local network and device data

Our remote control Apps work by talking directly to a television or streaming device on the same Wi-Fi network as your phone. To do that, an App may read and store the following on your device only:

  • The local IP address, port, device name, model and unique device identifier broadcast by the TV or streaming device you choose to control;
  • Pairing tokens, access keys or session certificates issued to your phone by that device so it does not have to be re-paired every time;
  • Your in-app preferences — saved devices, favourite apps or channels, button layout, theme, haptic and sound settings.

This information is never uploaded. It is written to your phone's private application storage, it is not transmitted to Zentrovaapps or to any third party, and it is deleted when you remove the saved device or uninstall the App. We do not operate any remote-control relay server: commands travel from your phone directly to your device across your own network.

8. Advertising and your ad choices

Our Apps display banner, interstitial, native and rewarded advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. Ads may be personalised (selected using your advertising ID and inferred interests) or non-personalised (selected using only coarse contextual signals such as country and the content of the App).

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App will show a consent message on first launch using Google's User Messaging Platform, in line with Google's EU User Consent Policy and the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. You may choose to consent to personalised advertising or to refuse it, and you can change your decision at any time from the App's Settings → Privacy options screen. If you refuse, you will continue to see ads, but they will be non-personalised.

Controlling your advertising ID

  • Open Settings → Google → All services → Ads on your Android device;
  • Select Delete advertising ID to stop apps from receiving it, or Reset advertising ID to disassociate it from past activity;
  • You can also opt out of personalised advertising across Google services at adssettings.google.com.

Removing ads entirely

Every App offers a paid option that removes advertising. Once an ad-free entitlement is active, no ad requests are made and no advertising data is collected by AdMob for that App. See the Subscription Policy for details.

9. In-app purchases and subscriptions

Our Apps offer optional in-app purchases, including auto-renewing subscriptions, a free trial period on selected plans, and a one-time lifetime purchase. All payments are processed exclusively by Google Play.

  • We never see, receive, handle or store your card number, bank details, billing address or any other payment credential. These are handled entirely by Google under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Google provides our App with a purchase token, product identifier and entitlement status so the App can unlock the features you paid for and restore them if you reinstall.
  • Your subscription status is validated against Google Play and cached on your device. We do not maintain a customer database linking purchases to identifiable individuals.

Full terms covering renewal, cancellation, price changes and refunds are set out in our Subscription Policy.

10. How information is used

The limited information described in this policy is used only to:

  • Discover, pair with and send commands to the device you choose to control;
  • Remember your saved devices and preferences between sessions;
  • Display advertising that funds the free version of the App;
  • Deliver, restore and manage purchases and subscriptions;
  • Diagnose faults reported to us by email and improve reliability and performance;
  • Prevent fraud, abuse and violations of our Terms of Use;
  • Comply with applicable law and enforce our legal rights.

We do not use your data for automated decision-making producing legal or similarly significant effects.

11. Legal bases for processing

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — for personalised advertising, and for optional permissions such as microphone access. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — to provide the App's core functionality and to deliver purchases and subscriptions you have bought.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — to serve non-personalised advertising, to keep the Apps secure and free from fraud, and to fix defects. We balance these interests against your rights and freedoms.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) — where processing is required by law.

12. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. Information is disclosed only in these situations:

  • Service providers — Google, in its capacity as advertising and payments provider, as described in sections 7 to 9;
  • Legal requirement — where we are compelled by a valid order of a court or competent authority, or where disclosure is necessary to comply with applicable law;
  • Protection of rights — where reasonably necessary to investigate fraud or abuse, or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims;
  • Corporate transaction — in the event of a merger, acquisition or transfer of assets, in which case you will be notified through the App or this website before any information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

14. Security

We apply measures appropriate to the very limited data our Apps handle. Data stored on your device is held in the App's private storage area, which other applications cannot read. Where a device protocol supports encryption, our Apps use the encrypted transport (for example TLS or the device's own secure pairing channel). Communication with advertising and billing services uses HTTPS.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your device, its screen lock and your Wi-Fi network secure.

15. Children's privacy

Our Apps are general-audience products intended for users aged 13 and over and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the minimum age of digital consent in your jurisdiction, which is up to 16 in parts of the EEA).

None of our Apps participates in the Google Play Families programme. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact support@zentrovaapps.com and we will delete it promptly.

16. International transfers

Zentrovaapps is established in Pakistan. Our advertising and billing providers process data on infrastructure located in the United States, the European Union and other countries. Where personal data of EEA or UK users is transferred outside those territories, Google relies on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and equivalent UK transfer mechanisms. By using the Apps you acknowledge that this limited technical data may be processed in countries whose data protection laws differ from those of your own country.

  • Restrict or object to processing, including objecting to direct marketing and profiling;
  • Data portability;
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal;
  • Lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

California residents

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, to correct inaccurate information, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. We do not sell personal information. Sharing of an advertising identifier for cross-context behavioural advertising can be stopped by refusing personalised ads in the App, deleting your advertising ID, or purchasing the ad-free option. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

How to exercise your rights

Because our Apps hold no account identifying you, most rights are exercised directly on your device using the controls described in sections 8 and 13. For anything else, write to support@zentrovaapps.com. We respond to verifiable requests within 30 days. There is no charge unless a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive.
