1. Who we are and what this covers

This Privacy Policy is issued by Zentrovaapps (SMC-Private) Limited ("Zentrovaapps", "we", "us" or "our"), a Single Member Private Limited company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2017 of Pakistan and registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan under Incorporation Number 0344416.

This Privacy Policy applies generally to mobile applications, games, puzzle games, utility applications, entertainment applications, remote-control applications and other digital products that we publish or distribute through Google Play and other supported app stores (collectively, the "Apps").

Our Apps may include current products as well as future applications and games. The features, permissions, data practices and third-party services used by an individual App may differ depending on its functionality.

Because different Apps may provide different features, not every section of this Privacy Policy applies to every App.

Where an individual App collects, accesses, stores or shares information in a way that is different from the general practices described in this Privacy Policy, the applicable information may also be disclosed within that App, its store listing, its Google Play Data Safety section, consent notices, or another appropriate notice.

You should review the information and permissions presented by the specific App you are using.

2. Privacy at a glance

Our Apps are designed to process only the information reasonably necessary to provide their features, maintain functionality, support users, process purchases, display advertising where applicable, protect our services and comply with applicable laws.

Some Apps may operate primarily on your device and may not require you to create an account or directly provide personal information.

Depending on the specific App and the features you use, information may be processed:

  • locally on your device;
  • by Zentrovaapps when you voluntarily contact us or use a feature that requires our services;
  • by Google Play when you download an App or make an in-app purchase;
  • by Google AdMob and applicable advertising partners when an App displays advertisements; or
  • by other third-party services specifically used by an individual App.

3. Information we generally do not collect directly

Zentrovaapps does not generally require users to create an account or provide personal information simply to use an App.

Depending on the App, we generally do not directly collect or request:

  • Your name, email address, postal address, phone number or date of birth unless you voluntarily provide such information, for example when contacting support.
  • User accounts, passwords or authentication credentials unless a particular App explicitly provides account functionality.
  • Precise geographic location from GPS or location services unless an individual App specifically requires such functionality and discloses it.
  • Your contacts, calendar, call logs or SMS messages unless a specific App feature requires access and clearly discloses that requirement.
  • Health, financial, biometric or other sensitive personal information unless explicitly required for a particular feature and disclosed separately.

We do not sell personal information to data brokers.

However, third-party services integrated into an App, such as advertising services, may independently process technical or advertising-related information as described in this Privacy Policy and their own privacy policies.

5. Games and gameplay information

Some Zentrovaapps Apps are games or puzzle applications, including puzzle and casual games.

Depending on the particular game, the App may store gameplay-related information on your device, such as:

  • Game progress;
  • Completed puzzles, levels or challenges;
  • Scores and statistics;
  • Difficulty preferences;
  • Game settings and preferences;
  • Saved game information; and
  • Other information necessary to provide the game's features.

Where gameplay information is stored locally on your device, it is not automatically transmitted to Zentrovaapps merely because it is stored by the App.

Some future or current games may provide online features such as leaderboards, achievements, cloud synchronization, multiplayer functionality or other connected features. If an App provides such a feature, information necessary to provide that feature may be processed by Zentrovaapps or the applicable third-party service.

Not every game uses online gameplay, cloud synchronization, leaderboards or game-service functionality.

6. Local device and network data

Certain Zentrovaapps Apps, particularly remote-control applications, may communicate with compatible televisions, streaming devices or other hardware on the same local network as your device.

Depending on the App, information processed locally may include:

  • Local network information;
  • The IP address, name, model or identifier of a compatible device that you choose to connect to;
  • Pairing information, access keys or session information required to maintain a connection; and
  • Your saved devices and in-app preferences.

Remote-control functionality:

Where an App communicates directly with a compatible device on your local network, commands may travel directly between your phone and the selected device. We do not automatically receive those commands merely because the App is being used.

The exact network permissions and connection methods depend on the particular remote-control App.

This section does not mean that every Zentrovaapps App accesses local network information. Game and utility applications may not require these capabilities.

10. In-app purchases and subscriptions

Some Zentrovaapps Apps offer optional in-app purchases, subscriptions, premium features, additional game content, ad removal or other paid functionality.

Purchases made through Android Apps are processed through Google Play Billing.

  • Zentrovaapps does not receive or store your complete payment card number or banking credentials through the App.

  • Depending on the implementation, this may include information such as a product identifier, purchase token and purchase or subscription status.
  • The exact purchase options and subscription plans vary between Apps.

Additional information about subscriptions, renewals, cancellations, purchases and refunds is provided in our Subscription Policy .

12. Legal bases for processing

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation applies, the applicable legal basis for processing depends on the type of processing and the particular App.

  • Consent — where consent is required for a particular processing activity, such as certain forms of personalised advertising.
  • Performance of a contract — where processing is necessary to provide a feature or service you requested, including applicable purchases or subscriptions.
  • Legitimate interests — where processing is reasonably necessary for purposes such as security, fraud prevention, maintaining services or improving reliability, while considering applicable user rights.
  • Legal obligation — where processing is necessary to comply with applicable law.

The applicable legal basis may differ depending on the particular App, service and jurisdiction.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device, operating-system account and network.

The intended audience, content rating and applicable child-safety requirements may differ between Apps.
