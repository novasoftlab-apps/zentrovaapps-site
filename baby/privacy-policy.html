Privacy Policy

How BabyTrack handles parent, caregiver, and baby information, including local records, advertising, and purchases.

1. Operator and scope

BabyTrack is a baby care tracking app published by Zentrovaapps (SMC-Private) Limited (“Zentrovaapps,” “we,” “us”), a company incorporated in Pakistan (Incorporation No. 0344416). This policy applies specifically to BabyTrack for Android. It supplements the privacy information shown within the app and on its Google Play listing.

BabyTrack is intended for parents and adult caregivers. It is used to record information about a baby, so we treat those records as sensitive even when they stay on your device. The data practices described here should be read together with the actual features and consent choices in the version of the app you use.

3. How data is used

  • Display and calculate care logs, timers, trends, reports, and reminders on your device.
  • Send local notifications for reminders if you enable them and grant Android notification permission.
  • Display and measure ads where AdMob is enabled, subject to applicable privacy choices.
  • Process and restore purchases through Google Play where paid features are enabled.
  • Answer support requests and meet legal or security obligations.

BabyTrack’s logs are not medical records maintained by a healthcare provider. We do not use a baby’s name, photo, medication notes, or care-log contents to build an advertising audience.

4. Advertising and consent

Where BabyTrack displays ads, it uses Google AdMob. Google’s Mobile Ads SDK can collect and share technical information such as IP address, app interaction and diagnostic information, and device identifiers for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on the app’s settings, your location, and your choices. Non-personalized ads may still use identifiers or local storage for limited purposes such as frequency capping and fraud prevention.

Where required in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, the app must request applicable advertising consent through a Google-certified consent management platform before personalized ad processing. You may also manage the Android advertising ID in device settings and Google ad preferences. If the app provides an in-app privacy options control, you can use it to revisit your choices.

Google’s information is available in its Privacy Policy and partner-services explanation. This policy does not replace any consent message shown in the app.

5. In-app purchases and subscriptions

If BabyTrack offers paid features, Google Play handles the purchase flow and payment information. We receive or check the minimum purchase and entitlement information needed to provide paid access and support. The available products, current prices, billing periods, trials, and renewal terms are shown before purchase in the app and Google Play checkout. See our Subscription and Purchase Policy for cancellation, renewal, and refund information.

6. Sharing and disclosures

We do not sell baby profiles or care-log contents. Information may be handled by: Google AdMob for ads in ad-enabled builds; Google Play for app distribution and purchases; your chosen share target, storage provider, or email app when you export or share a report; and our mailbox provider when you contact support. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect legal rights.

AdMob’s processing may qualify as “sharing” under some privacy laws even if we receive no money for individual records. Applicable opt-out rights are described below. We will identify any additional SDK or service that materially changes these practices before it is used.

7. Your controls

  • Edit or delete local records: use the app’s editing controls, or open Settings → Data management → Clear care records / Delete all local data. This removes local BabyTrack profiles and care records from the app on that device.
  • Export: use Reports → Export to save a PDF or CSV. Be careful where you save or share files containing a baby’s information.
  • Notifications: disable reminders within the app or revoke notification permission in Android settings.
  • Ads: use any in-app privacy options, your Android advertising controls, and Google’s ad settings, where applicable.
  • Purchases: manage subscriptions in Google Play Subscriptions.

Uninstalling the app normally removes its local storage from that device. Copies you exported, shared, or saved to a device backup are separate and may need to be deleted there. Because the current app does not create a Zentrovaapps account, there is no server account to delete for this version.

8. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them in the app, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. Android backup copies may remain under your Google or device backup settings. Google retains advertising and purchase information according to its own policies. We retain support messages only as long as reasonably needed to answer them, address a dispute, or comply with law; you can ask us to delete a support message when applicable.

If a future version adds a BabyTrack account or cloud storage, we will provide account and associated-data deletion controls in the app and an external request route as required by Google Play, and update this policy before launch.

9. Security

Current care data is kept in Android app storage and is subject to the security of your device and any backups you enable. The app does not transmit care logs to a Zentrovaapps server in the current version. Third-party services such as Google use their own safeguards for data they process. No device or service can be guaranteed completely secure; use a device lock and protect files you export.

10. Children’s privacy

BabyTrack is designed to be operated by adults caring for children, not by children themselves. Adult users may enter a child’s personal and health-related information to use the app. We do not intentionally ask children to use the app or contact us. If you believe a child has sent information directly to us, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

11. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain information we hold about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You can manage data stored only on your device using the controls above. For support data or other information we may hold, email info@zentrovaapps.com. We may verify a request before acting. You may also contact your local privacy regulator where applicable.

12. Changes

We may update this policy when the app, SDKs, or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version. Material changes will be communicated in the app or by another appropriate method where required.
