Why our apps

Built small, fast and private

Automatic discovery

Devices on the same Wi-Fi are found in seconds using standard discovery protocols. Tap once to pair.

Local-only control

Commands travel from your phone straight to your TV across your own network. We operate no remote-control servers.

Instant response

Native protocol implementations rather than generic bridges, so button presses land without lag.

Full keypad & keyboard

D-pad, volume, playback, channel keys and on-screen text entry so you can search without hunting letter by letter.

Free, with an honest upgrade

Every app is free and ad-supported. An optional purchase removes ads and unlocks premium features — never a paywall on basic control.

No account, ever

No sign-up, no email, no profile. Your settings stay on your device.
